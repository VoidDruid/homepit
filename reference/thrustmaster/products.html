OFFICIAL GRAN TURISMO RACING WHEEL

T-GT III brings together a Direct Axial Drive wheel base, the iconic T-GT wheel rim, and the Raceline 2-Pedals Load Cell pedal set. Officially licensed and developed in collaboration with Polyphony Digital and PlayStation, this bundle is fully optimized for Gran Turismo 7. Designed for the most demanding sim racers, T-GT III stands as the benchmark equipment to deliver the complete Gran Turismo experience. A comprehensive pack, compatible with PS5, PS4 and PC (Windows 10/11).

DIRECT AXIAL DRIVE TECHNOLOGY

Built on the proven Direct Axial Drive technology — introduced with T598 and refined through successive iterations — T-GT III’s base offers next-level reliability and performance, ensuring ultra-low latency (5 ms) and seamless rendering of in-game physics. With 5 N⋅m of constant torque and up to 10 N⋅m of overshoot capability, it delivers ideal power without saturation, allowing drivers to feel every loss of grip with surgical precision and instant responsiveness.

RACE DASH AND IMMERSIVE EFFECTS

Maintain full control on the track with the Race Dash display: view real-time telemetry (speed, RPM, lap times) and instantly adjust your Force Feedback settings. Its HARMONY technology generates high frequencies to transmit every track detail with zero cogging. T-GT III features all-new dynamic haptic effects adjustable on the fly (GEAR JOLT, ENGINE ROAR) for enhanced immersion. Its reinforced DC power connector and redesigned USB-C port guarantee robust reliability and longevity.

ICONIC T-GT WHEEL RIM

T-GT III features the wheel rim inherited from the T-GT range, preserving the iconic experience optimized for Gran Turismo. Its round rim (11 inches / 28 cm), wrapped in smooth, fine synthetic leather, draws inspiration from real sports cars to deliver absolute comfort during long sessions. Ideal for performance driving and drifting, it combines versatility with optimal grip to provide high-end feel, tailored to the game’s wide variety of challenges and vehicles.

INSTANT IN-GAME ADJUSTMENTS

The wheel features 4 metal rotary encoders with push-button functionality, providing 12 controls to adjust your settings in the heat of the action. The functions are automatically mapped in Gran Turismo (traction control, brake balance, engine mapping, torque distribution) and fully customizable on PC. Two mini-sticks facilitate menu navigation, while two metal paddle shifters ensure crisp, precise gear shifts.

LOAD CELL PEDAL SET

T-GT III includes the high-end Raceline 2-Pedals pedal set, featuring an all-metal structure and a 220 lb / 100 kg (24-bit) Load Cell sensor that replicates every gram of pressure for precise, intuitive braking. Customize your braking feel via 4 spring configurations and a preload dial, and adjust the response curve and dead zones directly via the Race Dash display. Its modular design seamlessly adapts to any setup, whether desk or cockpit mounted.

OFFICIAL FERRARI SF1000 REPLICA

Proudly represent the Scuderia in the premier class of motorsport with Formula Wheel Add-On Ferrari SF1000 T-Chrono Edition: a faithful 1:1 scale replica of the steering wheel from the Ferrari SF1000, the 2020 season single-seater race car, developed in collaboration with Ferrari and featuring the official markings for total immersion.

BUILT-IN LCD DISPLAY, PLUS LEDs

This replica features an interactive 4.3-inch / 109-mm IPS LCD display and 21 LEDs. The display can show up to 69 pieces of information* via different skins that adapt to the type of game being played. The LED array consists of 15 lights for the engine speed and 2 x 3 lights for race marshal flags.

NEXT-LEVEL IMMERSION

The wheel features up to 25 action buttons with the iconic labeling and markings (CHR, RADIO, PC). The 7 encoders — located near the user’s thumbs, the display, and the lower part of the wheel — make it easy to assign commands and intuitively control the car while making adjustments during races.

DESIGNED FOR PERFORMANCE

Formula Wheel Add-On Ferrari SF1000 T-Chrono Edition features a 100% carbon fiber central faceplate (21 layers, 3 mm thick in total) combining rigidity and lightweight construction, for balanced Force Feedback. The textured rubber grips faithfully reproduce the real steering wheel’s handling, ensuring exceptional comfort and control during races.

RESPONSIVE PUSH/PULL PADDLE SHIFTERS

The wheel features aluminum T-Chrono Paddles paddle shifters with a push/pull system, making shifting gears easy regardless of the wheel’s position. Gold-plated silver contact switches ensure lightning-fast gear shifting, with a response time of less than 5 ms. Two programmable analog levers are located at the back of the wheel.

VERSATILE COMPATIBILITY

Compatible with PS5, PS4 and PC (Windows 11/10), with the TS-XW, T-GT, T300, TX and TS-PC bases, and also with the T598 and T818 bases by using Quick Release Upgrade and Quick Release Adapter (sold separately).
The complete list of compatible games and features is available on the Thrustmaster support website.

OFFICIAL ACE COMBAT 8: WINGS OF THEVE LICENSE

Take off directly from the deck of an aircraft carrier with T.FLIGHT HOTAS NEO ACE COMBAT 8: WINGS OF THEVE Edition, the officially licensed Ace Combat 8 joystick. Thanks to its Plug & Play compatibility and optimized mapping, connect it to your XBOX Series X|S, XBOX One or PC and take off right away. Each control is designed to deliver a smooth, intuitive and fully immersive piloting experience.

FEEL ALL THE THRILLS OF DOGFIGHTING

Don’t just watch your battles — really feel them. The built-in vibration motor accurately delivers the sensations of missile launches, cannon fire, intense acceleration (G-forces), damage taken and many other in-game effects directly to your hand. Turn every mission into a unique sensory experience and feel the intensity of aerial combat as if you were at the controls of a real fighter jet.
*Vibration available only on XBOX Series X|S and PC

IMMERSION AT YOUR FINGERTIPS

Gain the upper hand in every battle with a layout designed for fighter pilots. With 7 control axes, 16 action buttons, a mini-stick, a rapid-fire trigger, a hat switch and a dedicated High-G Turns button, all the essential controls are right at your fingertips. Chain together maneuvers, lock onto your targets and fire without taking your hand off the stick, for smooth, intuitive and immersive piloting.

PRECISION THAT WON’T LET YOU DOWN

Enjoy incredibly precise piloting thanks to 16-bit resolution, which faithfully reproduces your every movement for enhanced control of your aircraft. To ensure long-lasting performance, the Z-axis features Thrustmaster’s H.E.A.R.T. (HallEffect AccuRate Technology) magnetic technology. With no mechanical contact, it eliminates the risk of drifting caused by wear and tear and ensures smooth, consistent control, mission after mission.

CUSTOMIZE YOUR COCKPIT

Configure your setup however you like, thanks to the detachable throttle. Keep the HOTAS as a single unit for a compact and stable setup, or separate the joystick from the throttle to experience the sensations of a real cockpit. Whether you’re playing on a desk, a table, or with the controls resting on your lap, enjoy outstanding comfort and total freedom of movement to successfully take on even the most intense missions back-to-back.

MASTER THE SKIES

Choose the piloting mode that suits you best. Control your aircraft’s rotation by twisting the stick thanks to its Z-axis, or use the dedicated lever on the detachable throttle. Two piloting modes, one mission: to provide every pilot with natural, precise and immersive control.

THE ESSENTIALS OF FLYING, ALL-IN-ONE

TCA SKY YOKE combines 7 axes, a built-in throttle and rudder controls in a single device. Its trim wheel provides smooth, progressive movement that feels more like the real thing than simple button adjustments. Precisely adjust the aircraft’s trim and stabilize your flight without having to hold the yoke in position.

REALISM IN EVERY MOVEMENT

Whether you’re piloting a light aircraft, a business jet or an airliner, enjoy realistic handling thanks to dimensions similar to those of a real aircraft yoke. Push or pull the yoke over a 3.5-inch/9-cm travel range to manage climbs and descents, then rotate it up to 180° to control your turns. The pitch axis is mounted on a steel shaft and features a self-centering damping mechanism (without a central detent and with a minimal dead zone) for smooth, progressive control.

YOUR AIRCRAFT, NATURALLY UNDER CONTROL

From takeoff to landing, quickly access the essential functions. The 24 action buttons, 8-way Point of View hat switch, mini-stick and two analog triggers are ergonomically positioned for instant access. Keep your hands on the yoke and your full attention on piloting, no matter the flight phase.

PRECISION ON ALL AXES

Enjoy precise control thanks to 16-bit resolution across all 7 axes, which faithfully reproduces every adjustment you make. Each axis also features Thrustmaster’s H.E.A.R.T. (HallEffect AccuRate Technology) magnetic technology. With no mechanical contact, it eliminates the risk of drifting caused by wear and tear and ensures smooth, consistent control, flight after flight.

PILOT AS A DUO, AND FLY TOGETHER

With the Dual Control, connect two TCA SKY YOKE controllers to the same PC to share the controls of the same aircraft. The instructor guides the student through the learning process, while the pilot and co-pilot can take turns at the controls during flight. Transfer control from one yoke to the other with the push of a button, then announce: “You have the controls.”

PLUG IN AND TAKE OFF

Enjoy an immersive piloting experience with TCA SKY YOKE in Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 on PS5 and PC. It’s also compatible with the Thrustmaster TFRP rudder pedals set (sold separately) to complete your setup and precisely control your aircraft’s yaw.

YOUR PRECONFIGURED CONTROLS FOR Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024

Find the TCA SKY YOKE mappings for free in the Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 Marketplace. Need help? Check out our installation guide to download and install them, and start flying.

Important information about Plug & Play mapping

Information regarding “Plug & Play” mapping for Euro Truck Simulator 2 and American Truck Simulator

Please be aware that the automatic “Plug & Play” profile for the SimTask Truck-40 Add-On Kit is not yet active by default in Euro Truck Simulator 2 and American Truck Simulator. Our teams have been working closely with developer SCS Software to roll out automatic control assignment, which will be included in an upcoming game update.

In the meantime, while awaiting the game update that includes this feature by default, you can ensure your wheel works seamlessly by applying the official Euro Truck Simulator 2 / American Truck Simulator profile created by SCS Software. This file is available to download from our support website under the “Farming / Trucking” section, alongside instructions on how to add it to your game files.

Alternatively, if you prefer to assign the controls manually, the detailed “Plug & Play” mapping is also available in the same section. As a reminder, please ensure your wheel base firmware is fully updated as well.

Thank you for your patience and understanding.

TRUCK KIT

Your driver’s seat awaits with SimTask Truck-40 Add-On Kit. This pack, specifically designed for driving trucks, combines a large-diameter wheel rim with its dedicated mounting system. By letting you position your racing wheel base horizontally, it instantly turns your desk into a real professional truck cab. All you have to do is hit the road!

ICONIC DESIGN

Take control with SimTask Truck-40 Add-On Kit’s large wheel rim (15.7 inches / 40 cm in diameter). Its realistic design is directly inspired by the most iconic European trucks. Designed for total immersion, it features authentic markings and an ergonomic layout that ensure smooth, intuitive access to all of the truck’s controls — without ever taking your eyes off the road.

PLUG & PLAY CONTROLS

Your wheel rim puts a full dashboard right at your fingertips with its 22 action buttons, including a mini-stick, 2 rocker switches, a mini turn signal control built into the back of the wheel rim, and an authentic horn button built into the faceplate. To make your journey easier, all these controls are automatically assigned via Plug & Play in Euro Truck Simulator 2 and American Truck Simulator*. Connect your equipment and focus on your trip!

ADJUSTABLE POSITION

Customize your driving position with SimTask Truck-40 Add-On Kit’s super-versatile mounting system. Designed to faithfully replicate the tilt angle of truck steering wheels, it lets you position your racing wheel base horizontally. Take advantage of four height presets and a 30° adjustable tilt angle to create a custom setup optimized for your comfort on long drives.

RIGID MOUNT

Set up your equipment with total confidence thanks to the mounting system’s ultra-rigid and durable 100% metal structure, crafted of 0.2 inch / 4 mm-thick sheet metal. Its easy 3-piece assembly ensures quick installation, while height and tilt adjustments are completely tool-free. It’s great for use on any non-glass desk between 0.8 – 2.8 inches / 2 – 7 cm thick.

NEXT-GENERATION MECHANISM

The WARTHOG MKII base’s construction has been completely redesigned and incorporates anti-stiction ball bearings, for enhanced durability and smoothness. This mechanism is paired with a mechanical alignment feature that ensures the grip is mounted correctly.

OPTIMIZED METAL STRUCTURE

The WARTHOG MKII base features a 100% metal structure that has been optimized in relation to the previous version. Sturdier and more durable, it delivers both smoother movements and a longer lifespan for the base.

MODERN ELECTRONIC ARCHITECTURE

The WARTHOG MKII base’s electronic architecture has been updated to ensure full compatibility with all generations of Thrustmaster grips. Switch grips instantly and choose from a wide range of options in an extensive ecosystem to match the aircraft you’re using in your game. In addition, the base’s new 6-pin connector supports simultaneous management of up to 128 buttons and 8 axes for exceptional precision.

15 YEARS OF HALL EFFECT EXPERTISE

The WARTHOG MKII base incorporates H.E.A.R.T. (HallEffect AccuRate Technology) technology, the result of Thrustmaster’s 15 years of expertise in Hall effect magnetic sensors. This technology guarantees precision and reliability without any drifting over time, with 16-bit resolution to capture every micro-movement with total accuracy. Experience a level of precision that makes all the difference during the most demanding flight phases.

GREAT FOR ALL SETUPS

The WARTHOG MKII base adapts to any setup. You can mount the base directly in a flight simulation cockpit, or use the AVA PLATE metal support plate (sold separately) for outstanding stability on a desk or table.

ADVANCED T.A.R.G.E.T. PROGRAMMING SOFTWARE FOR PC

T.A.R.G.E.T. is software that lets you configure, program and test out your Thrustmaster controllers.
T.A.R.G.E.T. allows for full interaction between different Thrustmaster USB controllers, simplified simulator configurations, and grouping multiple controllers into a single profile. Last but not least, T.A.R.G.E.T. includes predefined profiles for popular games, and lets you create new profiles too!

OFFICIAL U.S. AIR FORCE REPLICA

Immerse yourself in an ultra-realistic flight experience with this replica officially licensed by the U.S. Air Force, precisely replicating the iconic controls of the A-10C Warthog. The 1:1 scale VIPER flight stick grip and the dual-lever A-10C throttle faithfully recreate the sensations of a real military cockpit, for authentic control and total immersion in every mission.

THE ICONIC VIPER FLIGHT STICK GRIP

Get your hands on the Viper controls with an official 1:1 scale replica, developed under license from the U.S. Air Force. Its 100% metal structure replicates the original handling sensations, guaranteeing total immersion in your games. Featuring 19 action buttons and an 8-way PoV hat switch, the Viper flight stick grip provides a flight experience that’s as close as possible to a real fighter pilot’s.

AUTHENTIC WARTHOG THROTTLE

Directly inspired by the dual throttle controls of the U.S. Air Force’s A-10C, the HOTAS Warthog throttle offers independent control of both engines, with a locking system that lets you attach or separate the two throttle levers according to your needs.
Integrated into a comprehensive control panel, it features faithfully reproduced essential controls, ensuring precision, immersion and realism.

EVOLUTION FROM THE BASE UP

The HOTAS WARTHOG MKII base has been completely redesigned with smoothness, durability and compatibility in mind. Its optimized metal structure ensures smoother movements in the center and increased durability thanks to integration of an anti-stiction ball bearing gimbal. In terms of electronics, the new design and its 6-pin connector offer full compatibility with all Thrustmaster grips, simultaneously supporting up to 128 buttons and 8 axes.

15 YEARS OF HALL EFFECT EXPERTISE

HOTAS WARTHOG MKII features H.E.A.R.T. (HallEffect AccuRate Technology) technology, the result of Thrustmaster’s 15 years of expertise in the field of Hall effect magnetic sensors. This technology guarantees precision and reliability over time without any drifting, with 16-bit resolution to capture every micro-movement with absolute fidelity. It’s a level of precision that makes all the difference during the most demanding flight phases.

T.A.R.G.E.T** ADVANCED PROGRAMMING SOFTWARE ON PC

This is a software suite that lets you test, configure and program Thrustmaster controllers.
T.A.R.G.E.T allows for full interaction between different Thrustmaster USB controllers, simplified configurations in simulators, and the use of a single profile for all controllers rather than as many profiles as there are controllers. Finally, T.A.R.G.E.T includes preset profiles for famous games — both recent and older — and also lets you create new profiles!
** Free, available for download at https://support.thrustmaster.com
